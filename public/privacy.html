About the App

Quranly is a digital Quran companion application designed to help Muslims and learners of the Quran engage with the Holy Quran in a meaningful and accessible way. The app provides users with access to the full text of the Quran in Arabic, along with translations in multiple languages, audio recitations by renowned Qaris, tafsir (Quranic commentary), and tools for bookmarking, tracking reading progress, and setting daily recitation goals.

Your Data

Quranly is built for personal use and spiritual growth. Depending on how you use the app, we may store reading preferences, bookmarks, playlists, listening progress, azkar counters, and account profile details to personalize your experience and sync across devices when you sign in.

Quranly does not sell personal data for advertising or marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

To deliver core features, Quranly may communicate with third-party services, including:

Firebase (Google) for authentication and optional cloud sync; MP3Quran / Quran audio providers for recitations and radio; AlQuran Cloud and Quran.com for Quran text, tafsir, and search; AlAdhan for prayer times and Qibla; HadithAPI for hadith collections; and Google Gemini when you use Ask AI (requires your own API key or configured key).

Those providers process requests according to their own privacy policies. You can request account/data deletion by contacting us.
